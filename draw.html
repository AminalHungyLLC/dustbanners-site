Dustbanners is a card game for two players. It is set in a dusty world of four groups that fight over a shared Frontier. It is made by one person, Kyle.

Here is the honest part. The card art you see now is AI-made placeholder art. It is labeled that way everywhere. The final card art, box art, card backs and logo will be made by paid human artists. No AI art will be in the finished game, printed or digital.

This will be paid work once the game is funded. Nothing is promised yet; for now we’re building a list. Signing up is not a job offer, and it does not promise any work, pay or date. It lets you tell us who you are, so we can reach out when we are ready.

Sign up

The sign-up form is on Google Forms. It asks for:

  • your name (a pen name is fine);
  • a link to a web page that shows your work (a link only, no files);
  • the art styles you work in;
  • your rates, or “open to discuss” (we are not asking you to cut your price);
  • if you like: what you would like to draw, anything you would rather not draw, which groups interest you, where you are or your time zone, how you found us, and an email for a reply.

Sign up on our Google Form

Privacy

  • What we keep: only the answers you type in the form. We do not collect anything else about you.
  • Where: Google Forms saves your answers in a private Google Sheet that only Kyle can open. They are not shown on any public page, and your answers are never posted.
  • How long: 12 months, then deleted, or sooner once the art is planned.
  • Your email: optional, and used only if Kyle chooses to reply. It is not copied into other files or pages.
  • Remove yourself: email hello@dustbanners.com and say “remove me”. We will delete your answers and tell you when it is done.
  • Not sold, not shared. We do not sell your details or share them.

Kyle reads every answer himself. Nothing you write is run as a command or posted anywhere. Google has its own privacy policy for its forms. Read our full privacy notice.
