Privacy notice

Last updated November 7, 2026.

Who we are

Dustbanners is made by Kyle Miller under the name AminalHungy. Contact: hello@dustbanners.com. Our business mailing address will appear here and in every news email once our news emails begin.

What we collect

Only what you choose to give us: your email if you sign up for news; your email address and message if you send us feedback by email; your answers on our feedback forms (playtest and website; an email there is optional and only used to reply); your name if you want to be credited as a playtester or sign a playtest release; photos or quotes only if you agreed.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. If you use the light/dark or motion switches, your own browser remembers those choices, and which “Try one turn” example you saw last, on your device; they are never sent to us. Our website host, GitHub Pages, logs each visitor’s IP address for security, under GitHub’s own privacy statement. After you submit the signup form, the confirmation pages are run by Kit, under Kit’s own cookies and privacy policy, not ours.

Artist sign-up

Our artist page links to a sign-up form on Google Forms. If you fill it in, Google keeps your answers for us in a private Google Sheet that only Kyle can open. We keep only what you type there: your name or pen name, a link to your work, your art styles, and, if you choose, your rates, what you would like to draw, which cards or characters you like, where you are, how you found us and an email for a reply. We use them only to think about you for art work and, if you gave an email, to reply. Your answers are never posted or shown on a public page. We keep them until the game launches. Then we ask if you want to stay on the list. If you say no, or do not answer, we delete them. Email hello@dustbanners.com and say “remove me”, and we will delete them sooner. Signing up is not a job offer. Google’s own privacy policy also applies to its forms.

Narrator sign-up

Our narrator call links to a sign-up form on Google Forms. If you fill it in, Google keeps your answers for us in a private Google Sheet that only Kyle can open. We keep only what you type there: your name or pen name and whether you want to be paid or do it for fun, and, if you choose, a link to a voice sample, what you would like to voice, which characters you would like to voice and how they sound, your rate, how to credit you, where you are, how you found us and an email for a reply. We use them only to think about you for voice work and, if you gave an email, to reply. Your answers are never posted or shown on a public page. We keep them until the game launches. Then we ask if you want to stay on the list. If you say no, or do not answer, we delete them. Email hello@dustbanners.com and say “remove me”, and we will delete them sooner. Signing up is not a job offer. Google’s own privacy policy also applies to its forms.

Print-and-play demo

When the free print-and-play demo is ready, you can download it from this website. Downloading it does not ask for your name or email, and we do not track who downloads it. Our website host, GitHub Pages, logs each visitor’s IP address, as it does for every page.

Why

To send news you asked for, to fix the game, to thank playtesters, and to keep records of who agreed to what.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not share it, except with the tools we use to run the project (our email service Kit, Google Forms and Google Sheets for the feedback forms and feedback sheet, the AI tools that help us sort feedback, Kickstarter, and our order and shipping partners), and when the law requires it.

Feedback and email to us

Kyle is a real person. He reads every message sent to hello@dustbanners.com and every feedback form answer himself, in full, and he replies himself.

AI tools help us sort and list feedback. They do not decide anything, and they never replace your words with a summary. Any note an AI tool makes is kept apart from your words. Kyle reads your whole message himself. We treat your message as plain text, not as instructions.

If you send us feedback by email, Kyle may add it to our private feedback sheet, with your exact words, your email address and his notes, so we can reply. He decides. We only replace any other email addresses and phone numbers in your message, including in a signature, with the word [removed]. Nothing else in your message is changed. Emailing us never signs you up for news or any list. Nobody is signed up automatically.

The feedback sheet is private. Only Kyle and our hello@ account can open it. Unless you tell us we may, we never put your email address, or your message word for word, on a public page, a post or in our public files. We do not post what people send us.

On a feedback form, an email address is optional and is only used to reply.

To be removed, email hello@dustbanners.com and say “remove me”. Kyle will delete your row from the sheet when he reads your request. The original email stays in our hello@ inbox unless you ask us to delete it too. A deleted row may stay in Google’s version history and in our backups until they roll off.

Email

You will only get Dustbanners news if you signed up. Signup sends one confirmation email, which currently uses Kit’s built-in postal address. We will replace that with our business mailing address before sending news emails. Every news email will include an unsubscribe link and our business mailing address. Unsubscribing takes effect within 10 business days, and we aim for right away.

How long we keep it

Your email stays on the list until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Emails you send us, with Kyle’s notes, are kept as long as we are improving the game, or until you ask us to delete them. Feedback answers are kept as long as we are improving the game. Signed playtest releases are kept as proof of your choices.

Your choices

Ask us any time to see, fix or delete what we have about you. We will do it, except that anonymous scoresheets may stay. Email hello@dustbanners.com.

Kids

Dustbanners is for ages 14 and up. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think we did, tell us and we will delete it.

Other companies’ tools

GitHub, our email service, Google, Kickstarter, and later our pledge manager and shipping partner have their own privacy policies.
